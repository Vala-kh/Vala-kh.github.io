About me

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Technical University of Berlin (TU Berlin), working within the NOMOS project at the Ecohydrology group. My research focuses on climate-driven shifts in hydrological and environmental systems, with particular emphasis on extreme events, changing disturbance regimes, and resilience under emerging climate conditions.

My background lies at the intersection of agroecosystem modelling, ecohydrology, groundwater dynamics, and climate-resilient land management. I work with process-based models, environmental datasets, and scenario-based analyses to investigate how water, soil, vegetation, and management interact across natural landscapes under climatic change.

I am particularly interested in non-stationary climate dynamics, ecosystem recovery trajectories, and the role of hydrological processes in shaping the ecosystem functioning, land-use resilience, and sustainable management strategies.
