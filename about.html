About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher working at the intersection of agroecosystem modelling, hydrology, groundwater dynamics, and climate-resilient land management.

My research focuses on understanding how water, soil, vegetation, management, and climate interact in managed landscapes. I use process-based models, environmental data, and scenario-based simulations to explore sustainable futures for agroecosystems under changing climatic conditions.

I am especially interested in how hydrological processes influence ecosystem functioning, greenhouse gas regulation, and long-term land management strategies in wetlands, grasslands, and agricultural systems.
