Current Position

Postdoctoral Researcher · Technische Universität Berlin

My current research focuses on the application of process-based modelling to investigate hydrological processes, groundwater dynamics, ecosystem responses, and climate-resilient land management under changing environmental conditions. Building on my previous work on wet grasslands, peatlands, and groundwater-driven ecosystem dynamics, I study how managed landscapes respond to shifts in climate and disturbance regimes associated with the “new normal,” including changing precipitation patterns, water availability, and extreme events.
